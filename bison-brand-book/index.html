02 / Mark

The animal, not the costume.

Primary mark is the full bison — hump first, head down, unhurried. The head lockup is reserved for tight squares: app icon, favicon, wax seal.

Primary — dark field
Primary — bone field
Head — app / seal

Clear space

Keep a quiet field equal to the height of the hump on every side. The mark never shares a corner with another logo.

Minimum size

Full mark: 120px digital / 35mm print. Head icon: 24px digital / 10mm print. Below that, use the wordmark alone.

Approved grounds

Iron, hide, bone, snow, and photography dark enough that gold still reads. Never on busy pattern, never on neon.

Color of the animal

Prairie gold on dark. Iron on bone. White only as a last-resort reverse. No gradients, shadows, or outlines.

08 / Rules

Do not make the animal smaller.

If a layout needs decoration, it needs less type. If a mark needs an effect, it is on the wrong ground.

Don't stretch

Never scale off-axis. The hump is the proportion. Distorting it turns a bison into a cartoon.

Don't rotate

The animal stands. It does not charge at 18 degrees for a sticker pack.

Don't recolor

Gold, iron, or bone. No team colors, no seasonal neons, no metallic mock-chrome.

Don't crowd

Busy grounds eat the silhouette. If the photo is loud, put the mark in a quiet plate.
